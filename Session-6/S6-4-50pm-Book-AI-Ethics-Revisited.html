The Five Lenses Model

Four lenses bring the relevant values of a concrete practice into view. The fifth turns them into design specifications.

Lens I

Core values

What is the practice’s raison d’être? Its qualifying values come first.

Lens II

Enabling values

Which other value categories make the practice possible, and how critical is each?

Lens III

Stakeholders’ core values

Which primary and secondary stakeholders have justified interests?

Lens IV

Worldview values

What personal and shared convictions does the team bring?
